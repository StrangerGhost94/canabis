# Cairn — brand and design decisions

## What it is
A marketplace for **licensed** cannabis stores in Canada. People shop across stores, order from one store at a time, and pay that store when the order is handed over after an ID check. The name stays: a cairn marks the right path, and every store on Cairn is verified before it can sell.

## Direction: "the counter"
Shopping for cannabis in Canada happens at a counter, with product on shelves behind it. The marketplace recreates that: every product sits on a **shelf tinted by its format** and is drawn as its **package**, coloured by brand. It gives the grid rhythm and warmth without leaves, smoke or lifestyle imagery, works for any product a store adds, and keeps the focus on facts (format, THC, CBD, size, price). Stores can upload a real pack shot to replace the drawing.

Directions set aside: luxury boutique (wrong for an everyday utility), dark "tech" (generic), lifestyle editorial (pulls toward restricted promotion), map-first delivery app (interchangeable with every courier app).

## System
- **Palette** — porcelain ground `#eef0f2`, white surfaces, indigo ink `#1f2148`, and **sun yellow `#ffc93c` reserved for anything that puts something in your cart** (add buttons, cart badge, primary checkout). Pine green `#1c7a4f` means licensed/verified. Ten muted shelf tints, one per format.
- **Type** — one family, Instrument Sans (variable width): condensed and tight for headlines, normal width for reading, tabular figures for prices and potency.
- **Shape** — pill buttons, soft 18px shelves, crisp 10px fields. Shadows only on things that float (dialogs).
- **Logo** — the three stones, now indigo on a sun-yellow tile, with a lowercase wordmark.
- **Motion** — one moment: the homepage format shelves rise in once. Everything else responds to the user (add-to-cart confirmation, sheets). Reduced motion respected.
- **Trust** — a green "Licensed store" check on every product and store; tap for the full licence record. "You buy from" blocks name the legal seller wherever money is mentioned.
- **Voice** — plain and specific. "Place order with Larchmont Supply", "Pay the store at handover", "Bring government ID".

## Mobile
Bottom tabs (Home, Shop, Cart with count, Orders, Account) within thumb reach and safe-area aware; two-column product grid; filters in a bottom sheet; consoles collapse to a drawer; 16px+ inputs so iOS never zooms.
