# Cairn — brand and design decisions

## The idea
A cairn is a stack of stones left on a trail to tell the next traveller *you're on the right path*. That is the entire job of this product: tell an adult in Canada that the store in front of them is licensed, current and honest about what it has.

So the brand isn't about cannabis. It's about **verification made visible**. No leaves, no smoke, no lifestyle imagery.

## Who it serves
- **Customers** — adults who want a legitimate store nearby and don't want to wonder whether a website is legal. They want certainty in seconds.
- **Retailers** — licensed operators who already carry heavy compliance burdens. They want a listing that rewards being legitimate.
- **Partners** — writers, guides and community voices who stake their own reputation on a recommendation. They want honest numbers and a profile that signals credibility.

Five seconds in, the platform should feel like a **public register written by a careful editor**: calm, precise, trustworthy, adult.

## Directions considered, and why this one
| Direction | Why not |
|---|---|
| Luxury / boutique | Signals exclusivity and price; wrong for a utility people must trust. |
| Lifestyle / editorial magazine | Pulls toward the "lifestyle" promotion the Cannabis Act restricts. |
| Map-first local discovery | Becomes interchangeable with every delivery app. |
| Dark "tech platform" | Generic; says nothing about verification. |
| **Field register (chosen)** | Grounds everything in the one thing only this product does: show the licence. |

## The system
- **The cairn mark** — three stones, each a check: top = licence verified against the regulator's registry, middle = licence current, base = availability fresh. It appears on every store, at every size, and opens the full record. Partial or outlined stones communicate pending, simulated or expired states without words.
- **Palette** — Canadian Shield granite (`#e3e6e1`) as ground, spruce (`#17302a`) as ink, lake blue (`#1f5672`) for links and data, and one signal colour borrowed from surveyors' **flagging tape** (`#d92c6c`) — used only for the top stone (verified) and the single most important action on a screen.
- **Type** — one family, Bricolage Grotesque (variable). Condensed and tight for display; normal width for reading; tabular figures for every number.
- **Shape** — controls are crisp (6px); only stones are soft and irregular. Few cards; most content sits on ruled lists, like a register.
- **Products** — no photography by default. Each product is drawn as a **label tile**: format, THC, CBD — echoing the standardised potency panel on Canadian packaging. Factual, comparable, and compliant.
- **Motion** — one moment: the hero cairn's stones settle on load. Everything else moves only in response to the user. Reduced motion respected.
- **Voice** — plain, specific, sentence case. Errors say what happened and what to do. No hype, no health claims, no stereotypical cannabis language.

## Mobile model
Bottom tab bar (Home, Discover, Saved, Account) within thumb reach and safe-area aware; filters live in a bottom sheet; consoles collapse to a top bar with a drawer. Inputs are 16px+ so iOS never zooms.
